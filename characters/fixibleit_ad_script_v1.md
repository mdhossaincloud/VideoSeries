# FIXIBLE IT — "এক জায়গায় সব সমাধান" (Ad Script v1, ~45s, 16:9)

**Objective:** Introduce FIXIBLE IT as the one-stop IT service for shops and homes (CCTV, Wi-Fi/networking, PC service).
**Audience:** Small shop owners and families in Bangladesh.
**Tone:** Warm, witty, relatable → relief → confident CTA.
**Cast:** রাফি (FIXIBLE IT technician), করিম ভাই (shop owner), নাসিমা আপা (mother), টুটুল (student), দাদু (grandfather).

**Word count:** ~95 Bangla words → fits 45–48s at natural pace.

### Notes / assumptions
- Service list (CCTV, Wi-Fi/networking, PC service) is taken from the business profile; fixibleit.com could not be checked from this environment — **confirm before voice recording.**
- No prices, warranties, response times or "24/7" claims are used.
- Line 7 ("মোবাইলেই দোকান দেখি") assumes FIXIBLE IT configures mobile remote viewing for CCTV — confirm.
- A phone/WhatsApp number can be added to the CTA if the business wants one.
